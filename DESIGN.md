# Design Specification – Evening Wire (18 Nov 2026)

**Palette**
- Background: #111418 (very dark gray‑blue)
- Primary text: #ffffff (white)
- Accent colour: #ffb000 (amber) – used for highlights, buttons and key numbers
- Tag colour: #00ffff (cyan) – for category tags and metadata

**Typography**
- Grotesque‑style sans‑serif (e.g., "Inter" or "Montserrat") for all copy.
- Large, bold masthead text.

**Layout**
1. **Masthead** – Full‑width bar at the top reading **"WEDNESDAY 18 NOVEMBER 2026"** in white, centred, with amber underline.
2. **Numbered Story Blocks** – Each headline appears in its own horizontal band (full‑width) numbered on the left (amber) and the story text on the right (white). Bands alternate subtle shades of the background for visual separation.
3. **World‑Map Panel** – A stylised world map on the right side of the page, 400 px wide, with numbered dots corresponding to the story blocks. Hover state (in UI) would highlight the related block.
4. **Ticker** – Bottom‑center scrolling ticker showing a concise 20+‑item brief summary of the day’s news (e.g., “Markets down, GTA VI out, Measles surge…”).
5. **Sources Footer** – Thin footer bar with small white text listing the source agencies in cyan, separated by vertical bars.

**Resolution & Export**
- Final canvas size: 1024 × 1024 px (square) to fit typical social‑media formats.
- All elements must remain legible at this size; use minimum 12 pt for body text, 24 pt for headlines.

**Design Intent**
The “Evening Wire” evokes a night‑time news broadcast: dark background for a calm, authoritative feel; amber accents draw the eye to key numbers; cyan tags add a modern, tech‑savvy touch. The numbered layout and map panel provide quick visual navigation across global stories.
