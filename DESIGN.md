# Design Specification – "Evening Wire"

- **Palette**: background #111418, primary text white, accent amber #ffb000, tag cyan.
- **Typography**: grotesque sans‑serif for all copy.
- **Masthead**: centered, "WEDNESDAY 18 NOVEMBER 2026" in large amber font.
- **Layout**: numbered story blocks stacked in horizontal bands; each band alternates subtle shade for readability.
- **World‑Map Panel**: stylized dark map with numbered dots correlating to story blocks.
- **Ticker**: scrolling strip at bottom showing 20+ brief headlines.
- **Footer**: sources list, small print, same amber accent.
- **Resolution**: 1024 × 1024 pixels, legible at typical screen distances.
